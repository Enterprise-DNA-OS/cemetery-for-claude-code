# PlotBox selection, 15 October 2026

**The price list.** PlotBox's own G-Cloud 15 pricing document for PlotBox Pro charges a monthly subscription per named user: £150 for Tier 1 administrative staff, £50 for Tier 2 (grounds staff, cremation technicians, receptionists) and £50 for Tier 3 management users, all plus VAT. Bolt-on modules (CRM, contracts, documents, digital mapping, funeral director portal, memorial mason portal, public portal, finance export) cost extra. The minimum onboarding fee for one cemetery or crematorium with three users and no data migration or mapping is £4,500 plus VAT. Source: [PlotBox Pro G-Cloud 15 pricing document](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/702569/762511737682905-pricing-document-2026-01-30-1015.pdf), checked today.

**What one cemetery district pays.** Riverside County's Board of Supervisors approved a five-year PlotBox agreement for the Perris Valley Cemetery District "in the annual amount of $7,980, and up to an additional $49,230 in initial start-up costs", US$89,130 in total. Source: [Riverside County Board of Supervisors, 25 June 2024, item 3.41](https://media.rivcocob.org/proceeds/2024/p2024_06_25_files/03.41001.pdf), checked today. This is one small US district, not a NZ or AU invoice: the start-up cost is six times the annual fee.

**Who uses it here.** Wellington City Council runs its cemeteries on PlotBox: two open cemeteries (Karori and Mākara) and two closed ones, with over 107,900 plot records. PlotBox has an office in Sydney. Source: [PlotBox news](https://plotbox.com/en-au/news/plotbox-partners-with-wellington-city-council).

**The way out.** PlotBox's G-Cloud listing says users can export reports and searches to CSV at any time, and that customer data is returned in an open format when a contract ends.

**The rules.** Burial and Cremation Act 1964 (NZ), sections 10, 50 and 51, quoted in `docs/compliance.md`.

Scores, in order: annual bill 3, data-shaped 5, our buyers 2, search demand 2, one-session buildable 5, import path 3. Total 20/30. The bill is per named user and per module, and a council the size of Wellington pays well above the small district above; neither publishes its invoice. The data is the cleanest shape in the queue's open families: plots, rights, a register and bookings. Buyer fit is the weak score: most cemeteries belong to councils, with cemetery trusts, private cemeteries and crematoria the closer fit. Search demand for "PlotBox alternative" is thin; the page also targets "cemetery management software" and "cemetery software nz".
